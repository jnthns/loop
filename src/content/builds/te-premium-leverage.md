The +0.5 TE premium in this league is modest compared to a full 1.0 or 1.5
bump some formats run, but stacked against a single dedicated `TE` slot (no
second flex-eligible TE start beyond the standard `FLEX`s) it is real value
concentrated in a shallow position — see
`src/content/knowledge/roster-construction/superflex-positional-builds.md` for
why that combination, and not the premium alone, is what makes this build
work here specifically.

The thesis only holds for a genuine every-down role. A tight end whose value
is touchdown-dependent redzone work rather than target volume does not
actually capture the premium the way the math promises — the +0.5 bonus
scales with receptions, not with scoring, so a low-target, high-touchdown TE
underperforms the archetype's own logic. That distinction is exactly why
coaching changes around the position matter more here than at other spots:
a new position coach, a scheme shift, or a depth-chart reshuffle can move a
tight end from "every-down" to "committee" faster than the market reprices
it, and this build has no depth to fall back on if that happens to your
anchor.

Spend one premium pick here, not two — chasing a second TE at premium cost
abandons the WR and RB need the rest of the roster still has, for marginal
extra edge at a position where one good starter already captures most of the
available value.
