Every ranking list you read was written for some format. If it is not yours, the
list is a rough draft at best. These are the settings that move value most, and
this league's current values are recorded in `data/team.json`.

## Superflex (this league: yes)

Starting a second quarterback is the largest single change a league can make.

- Up to two QBs per team start, so in 12 teams as many as 24 are startable —
  and there are not 24 startable quarterbacks.
- Quarterbacks move from "wait on one" to the most valuable asset class,
  especially young ones.
- The correct startup and trade behavior inverts: paying a first-round price for
  a young QB is normal, not a reach.
- Practical rule: roster three quarterbacks if you can. The third one is
  insurance against the exact week you would otherwise lose.

## TE premium (this league: +0.5 per reception)

TE premium takes a position with almost no usable depth and rewards the handful
of tight ends who catch real volume.

- The gap between TE1 and TE12 widens sharply.
- Reception-heavy tight ends gain more than touchdown-dependent ones — target
  volume is what the bonus multiplies.
- Rookie tight ends become better stashes, since the eventual payoff is larger.

## PPR (this league: 1.0)

Full PPR rewards volume over efficiency and lifts the floor of pass-catching
running backs and slot receivers.

- A back who catches 50 passes is a different asset than one who does not, even
  at identical rushing totals.
- Efficiency-driven profiles — big plays, few targets — carry more week-to-week
  variance.

## Roster and lineup depth

- **Deeper benches** raise the value of stashes and rookie picks, because there
  is somewhere to put them.
- **Shallower benches** make win-now production king and turn taxi/IR rules into
  a genuine competitive advantage.
- **More starting slots** flattens the value curve; fewer slots concentrate it
  at the top.

## The habit worth building

Before you evaluate any trade or ranking, restate your format in one line:
*12-team, superflex, 1 PPR, +0.5 TE premium, 20 roster spots.* Half of the bad
dynasty takes on the internet dissolve once you do.
