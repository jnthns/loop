`roster-construction/superflex-positional-builds.md` describes which builds work
in this format. This page narrows that to where you actually pick. You are the
**4 seed of 12 in a 30-round snake** (see `data/draft.json` — the app's draft
panel reads the same numbers), which sets your board before anyone speaks.

## Your picks, and the shape they make

| Round | Overall | Round | Overall |
| ----- | ------- | ----- | ------- |
| 1.04  | 4       | 6.09  | 69      |
| 2.09  | 21      | 7.04  | 76      |
| 3.04  | 28      | 8.09  | 93      |
| 4.09  | 45      | 9.04  | 100     |
| 5.04  | 52      | 10.09 | 117     |

Two facts fall out of that column, and everything below is downstream of them.

- **You wait 16 picks after your opener.** Longer than any of slots 1–3. The
  board you see at 2.09 is a genuinely different board from the one you left,
  and one full positional run can happen inside that gap without you.
- **Then you pick twice in seven.** 21 and 28 are close enough to function as a
  turn: whatever tier you enter at 2.09, you can usually still take from it at
  3.04. The same pairing repeats at 45/52, 69/76, 93/100 — every odd round's
  late pick sits seven ahead of the next even round's early one. You get nine
  of these paired windows before round 10.

The 1 and 12 seeds get one enormous swing each turn. You get a smaller swing,
five times, starting earlier. That is a structurally different draft, and it
rewards a different plan.

## What the 4 slot does to each build

### Robust QB — available, and cheapest at your pairs

The format-wide default (fix the QB room early) holds here, but the 4 slot
changes *where*. You will not have the top QB on the board at 1.04 unless three
managers ahead of you open at another position, which in superflex is unlikely.
Do not spend 1.04 chasing the QB1 you can't have.

Instead, treat 21/28 as the QB window. If the QB run starts in round 2, seven
picks between your selections means you can take one, watch four go, and still
take a second from the same tier at 3.04 — the exact move the 1 and 12 seeds
cannot make, because their next pick is 20+ away. Two startable QBs by pick 28
is the cleanest path this seat offers.

- *Works when:* the QB tier you enter at 2.09 is at least four deep.
- *Fails when:* you wait for 21 with a tier that has three left — the run
  clears it inside your 16-pick gap and you take a QB2 floor at a QB1 price.

### Hero RB — the natural 1.04 build

The 4 slot is where a workhorse RB is most often the honest best player
available: the elite QBs and the consensus WR1 typically go inside the first
three picks, and what's left at 4 is frequently the clear lead back. Taking him
does not cost you the QB room *if* you then commit 21 and 28 to quarterbacks.
Hero RB and Robust QB are not alternatives from this seat — they're the same
plan, one round apart.

- *Works when:* you actually spend 21 **and** 28 on QBs and don't get tempted
  by a falling WR.
- *Fails when:* 1.04 becomes a second RB by round 3, and you enter round 4
  needing two quarterbacks with the tier gone.

### Zero RB — hardest build from this seat

Zero RB wants a top QB *and* a WR room, and 1.04 gives you one premium asset,
not two. From the 12 slot you can double-tap the turn and have both; from 4 you
are asking picks 21 and 28 to carry both the QB room and the WR corps, which
the seven-pick pairing is too narrow to do. Viable only if the board hands you
an elite QB at 1.04 because managers ahead reached at RB.

- *Works when:* an unambiguous top-tier QB falls to 4.
- *Fails when:* you run it by default and start `FLEX` × 2 with dart throws.

### TE anchor — a 3.04 move, not a 1.04 move

The +0.5 premium is real, but 1.04 is too expensive for it when the RB/QB
board is this rich at 4. The pairing is the play instead: if the premium TE is
still there at 21, he is usually still there at 28, so take the position you're
scarcer at first and the tight end second. Let the pair do the arbitrage.

### Balanced / BPA — the trap that specifically catches this seat

The 16-pick gap punishes indecision more than any other seat's. Arriving at
2.09 without a decided direction means reacting to a board that moved 16 picks
since you last looked, twice in seven picks, and the pairing quietly turns into
two players at the same position you didn't need.

## The one-sentence version

From the 4 slot, take the best non-QB asset at 1.04 — usually the workhorse RB
— then treat 21 and 28 as a single two-QB decision, and keep using every
seven-pick pair the same way: enter a tier, and take from it twice.
